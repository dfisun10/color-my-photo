      03Good to know

      Questions

      What happens to my photos?

      They are resized in your browser and sent only to make your pages (via OpenAI's image service). We do not keep your photos. Until you download the full PDF, the resized photos stay saved in this browser only, so you can pay and come back. Finished pages are kept for 30 days so you can download again, then deleted.

      Is the watermark on my book?

      No. The PREVIEW stamp is only on the one free draft. The pages you pay for are clean.

      Which photos work best?

      One clear subject, good light, and a visible face. Pets, kids, toys, houses and cars all work. Busy group photos make busy pages.

      What if a page looks wrong?

      Use Remake on that page, or write to us for a refund.

      How long does it take?

      About 1 minute for every 2 pages, right after you pay. No waiting days for a seller.

      Can I print at home?

      Yes. The PDF is US Letter and prints fine on A4 too. Every page also comes as a PNG.
