05Good to know

Questions

Whose name will I see when I pay?

Checkout shows Create Once, the company behind Color My Photo. Your card statement may show Create Once or Link. Stripe handles the payment.

What happens to my photos?

They are resized in your browser and sent only to make your pages (via OpenAI's image service). We do not keep your photos. Until you download the full PDF, the resized photos stay saved in this browser only, so you can pay and come back. Finished pages are kept for 30 days so you can download again, then deleted.

Will it look like us?

It will look like a drawing of you. Each page is drawn from your photo, so it is a likeness and not an exact copy, and the result depends on the photo: one clear subject, good light and a visible face work best. That is why the first page is free. See yours before you pay.

What if a page looks wrong?

Use Remake on that page. If a page is wrong, failed, or you could not get your book, write to hello@getcolormyphoto.com within 14 days of your order: we remake the page, or refund your $12 in full if you prefer.

Can I print at home? Do you ship?

You print it. The PDF is US Letter and prints fine on A4 too, at home or at a print shop, and every page also comes as a PNG. We do not print or ship anything.
