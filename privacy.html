Privacy

Your photos. Your browser shrinks each photo before it is sent. We send it to OpenAI only to make your coloring page. We do not store your original photos. Until you download the full PDF, your photos stay in your own browser.

OpenAI. We send your photos to OpenAI to make the pages. OpenAI may keep API inputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deletes them.

IP address. We keep your IP address for up to 2 days, only to enforce the one-free-preview limit.

Your finished pages. We keep them for 30 days so you can download them again, then we delete them.

Payments. Stripe is the merchant of record: the seller of record, who handles payment, tax, your receipt and refunds. We never see your card details. Checkout shows Create Once, the company behind Color My Photo. Your card statement may show Create Once or Link.

Counting. We count page visits and button clicks with a free counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). We set no cookies and send it no personal data, but like any website it can see your IP address when your browser contacts it.

Refunds. Your book is made for you and delivered right away, so we do not refund a change of mind after your pages are delivered. If a page is wrong, failed, or you could not get your book, write to hello@getcolormyphoto.com within 14 days of your order: we remake the page, or refund your $12 in full if you prefer. A full refund closes the download link. Paid twice by mistake? Write to us and we refund the extra payment. Your order is sold through Link (Stripe), which may also decide refunds under its own terms; rights the law gives you where you live are not affected.
