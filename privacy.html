Privacy

What this page is. Color My Photo is an early-access test. The tool is not open yet, nothing is for sale and no payment is taken.

What we keep. Only the email address you type into the sign-up box. It is stored with Google, in Google Forms, under Google's own privacy terms.

What we use it for. To email you when the tool opens or the photo book goes on sale. We do not sell or share it.

Unsubscribe and deletion. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. To have your address deleted at any time, email hello@getcolormyphoto.com. If we decide not to launch, we delete the whole list.

Contact. Questions about Color My Photo: hello@getcolormyphoto.com.

Photos. This page does not upload or keep any photos.

Counting. We count page visits and button clicks with a free counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). We set no cookies and send it no personal data, but like any website it can see your IP address when your browser contacts it.
