Privacy

What this page is. Color My Photo is an early-access test. The tool is not open yet, nothing is for sale and no payment is taken.

What we keep. Only the email address you type into the sign-up box. It is stored in Google Forms.

What we use it for. To email you when the tool opens or the photo book goes on sale. We do not sell or share it.

Unsubscribe and deletion. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can reply to any of them to have your address deleted. If we decide not to launch, we delete the whole list.

Photos. This page does not upload or keep any photos.

Counting. We count anonymous page visits and button clicks with a simple counter. No cookies, and no personal data is attached.
